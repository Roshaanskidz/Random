Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This privacy policy applies to Pin2Sticker, a personal image-organization utility.

1. What the app does

Pin2Sticker helps a user find and organize image Pins from Pinterest for the user's personal use.

2. Pinterest account information

If Pinterest sign-in or authorization is used, Pin2Sticker may receive the Pinterest account information and permissions that Pinterest makes available through its authorization process. The app uses that access only to provide the features the user requests.

3. Images and Pin data

When the user selects Pinterest content, Pin2Sticker may process information needed to retrieve and organize the selected content. The app does not claim ownership of third-party content.

4. Sharing

Pin2Sticker does not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except where necessary to provide the requested service, comply with law, protect the service, or where the user directly chooses to share content.

5. Data retention and security

Data is retained only for as long as necessary for the app's functionality or as required by law. Reasonable security measures are used to protect information handled by the service.

6. Children's privacy

Pin2Sticker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
